Card Game: Maine Knight's Memoir

What drives a man to disappear into the forest—and stay hidden for 27 years? Step into the quiet, untamed world of Christopher Thomas Knight. In Maine: Knight’s Memoirs, you’ll walk the path of a man who turned his back on society in search of something deeper. Live the solitude. Face the unknown. Shape a story few ever knew existed.

Team Project: Bloody Good Curry

You are playing as Ming, a curry rice hawker store owner. You saw in an article about the Number One curry rice hawker store owned by Hei Bang. So, the next logical step is to steal the recipe for yourself, and become the Number One instead! But, as you tried to get the recipe for your own, you realized that the recipe uses human blood as the secret ingredient! And, a much more sinister force is at play here, what will you do next?
